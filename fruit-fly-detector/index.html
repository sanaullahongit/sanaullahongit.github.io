Bactrocera Fruit Fly Detector

Upload a photo and this page will try to detect and label Bactrocera dorsalis and Bactrocera zonata fruit flies in it, entirely in your browser — no image is uploaded to any server. Built on the YOLOv8 model from my M.Phil. thesis research, exported to ONNX and running via onnxruntime-web.

Click to choose an image or drag one here
JPG or PNG, ideally a trap-camera style photo
Loading model…

This model was trained and validated on a public trap-camera image dataset (Tariq et al., 2022) covering only these two species. It works best on trap-style photos similar to the training data — accuracy will vary on other kinds of images (e.g. pinned specimens or close-up studio photos), since those fall outside what the model was trained on.
